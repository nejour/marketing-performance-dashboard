How each number is worked out
Which leads are counted
Opportunities in the "Sales Pipeline" whose contact is tagged dash-project (the agency's leads). Test leads, demo/sandbox records and records added by other users are left out. Duplicate contacts (same email or phone) are counted once.
Leads
The number of agency leads that came from each source.
Pipeline
How many of each source's leads are in each stage today. The stages always add up to the source's lead total.
Funnel steps
Opted in: every lead. Booked a call: leads now in Booked call, Cancelled, No Show, Follow Up, Lost or Closed. Had the call: leads now in Follow Up, Lost or Closed. Closed: leads in Closed (they paid). Disqualified, Cancelled, No Show and Lost are exits, not steps forward.
Conversion %
Leads that reached a step divided by the leads that reached the step before it. Example: "58% (11 of 19)" means 11 of the 19 leads who booked a call actually had it.
Revenue
The sum of deal values in the Closed stage. A deal value more than 10 times the typical deal () is treated as a data-entry error: the lead still counts everywhere, but its value is left out of revenue. A closed deal with a $0 value counts as a sale with $0.
Revenue per lead
Revenue divided by the number of leads from that source. Used for the budget advice because there is no ad-spend data.
Freshness
Numbers are pulled from GoHighLevel when the page opens and reused for up to 15 minutes. Nothing in GoHighLevel is ever changed by this page.
